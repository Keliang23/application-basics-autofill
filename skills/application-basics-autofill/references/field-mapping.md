# Field mapping decisions

Read the current portal's instructions before applying these rules. Use official guidance when an ambiguity depends on institutional policy; do not invent a universal admissions rule.

| Field | Evidence and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Legal / preferred name | Distinguish legal, preferred, former, given, middle and family names. Use the relevant identity document and explicit user preference. Do not expand initials or infer a name split from word order alone. |
| Address | Preserve user-confirmed address components; transliteration is not permission to invent a district, building/unit meaning or postal code. Separate current mailing and permanent address. |
| Citizenship / residency / visa | Treat each as a separate question. Do not infer nationality, residency classification or immigration status from language, home address or university location. |
| Institutions attended | Check all relevant records and the form's reporting rule, including transfer, visiting, online and non-degree study. Transferred credit does not by itself exempt the original institution from disclosure. |
| Degree / major | Distinguish attendance, degree candidacy and an earned award. A course name, study mode or “non-degree” category is not automatically a major. Do not turn a short programme into a degree. |
| Dates | Distinguish course dates, academic term boundaries, attendance, employment, expected completion and actual conferral. Retain original precision; do not invent a day when only a month is known. An official calendar may establish term boundaries, not an individual's attendance. If the form demands greater precision, ask or locate further evidence. |
| GPA / grades / credits | Match the requested scale and institutional, cumulative, transfer or other basis. Preserve decimals and units. Do not calculate or convert unless required; when required, use the specified method and retain the original figure and calculation basis. |
| Tests / exemptions | Distinguish scheduled, completed, self-reported and officially received results. An apparent exemption criterion is not proof that the applicant has been granted a waiver. |
| Work / projects / activities | Verify role, organization, dates and contribution against designated records. Separate design exercises, prototypes, released features, team outcomes and personal contributions. Never invent hours, impact metrics or job seniority. |
| Recommenders | Verify supplied names, contacts and titles. Do not infer relationship, years known or teaching history from title alone. Check whether adding/saving a recommender sends an invitation before acting. |
| Personal declarations | Do not infer negative answers from missing records, including prior applications, conduct, criminal history, family education or other personal questions. Respect the exact question's scope and the applicant's explicit answer. |
| Non-essay factual short answers | Include all such questions in basics, regardless of word limit. Summarize verified duties, courses or other facts; record existing explicit choices rather than inventing preferences. Motivation and reflective narratives are separate essay-writing tasks. |
| Existing essay attachments | A completed SOP/PS file may be uploaded as part of basics. Verify the applicant, institution/programme, version, readiness and required format. Do not rewrite the file or choose between ambiguous drafts without clarification. |
| Save / upload / submit controls | Inspect actual effects. Saving drafts and uploading existing files are allowed when authorized and when they do not submit the application. Final submission is always applicant-only, even if the agent is explicitly asked to do it. Hand off combined submission actions as well. |

When two records conflict, note both sources privately, identify which field is affected, and resolve with the applicant or a relevant authoritative record. Leave the affected field pending while continuing the rest. An earlier draft remains a draft even if it contains a plausible answer.
